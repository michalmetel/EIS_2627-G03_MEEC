Support

Common questions

How do I send a parcel?

Open the order form and tell us where it's going and what's inside. You only need to log in at the end, and your order won't get lost when you do.

Where's my parcel?

Type your order number, like #2041, into the tracking box on the home page.

Can I send to another country?

Yes, as long as you can get there by road.

My order was cancelled. What now?

Don't worry, it isn't lost. It goes back on the list and another courier can pick it up.

I want to work as a courier

Great! Sign up here with your ID card and criminal record. Once we've had a look, you can log in and start taking orders.
