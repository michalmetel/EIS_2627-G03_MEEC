Common questions

How do I send a parcel?

Fill in the order form with the pick-up point, the destination and what you're sending. You log in when you submit it.

How do I find my order?

Enter its number, like #2041, in Track your shipment on the home page.

Can you deliver anywhere?

Anywhere a courier can drive to. The order form warns you if a point is in the water, far from a road or has no road route.

What if my order is cancelled?

It goes back on the list of orders so another courier can take it.

How do I become a courier?

Request a courier account and add your documents. Staff check them first, and each vehicle needs valid insurance and inspection.
